    How scoring works
    • Each chore pays the coins shown on its card. Bigger jobs pay more.
    • On-time bonus: +5 coins when you finish on or before the planned day.
    • Full house bonus: +50 coins for every week where every chore got done (dishes twice). "Other" is optional and never blocks the bonus.
    • "Other" is for anything extra you did that isn't on the list. It pays 10 coins once a week.
    • Week streak counts full-house weeks in a row. The current week counts once it's complete.
    • Levels come from all coins earned, not what you have left, so spending never lowers your level. Every 150 earned is a level.
    • Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
